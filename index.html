ScholarFix

Reference & formatting toolkit for academics — validate and repair DOIs, screen for retracted works, restyle citations, and format manuscripts for a target venue. No account, no server; your references never leave your browser except for the metadata lookups shown below.

DOI Fixer

Paste references (one per line) or upload a .txt, .bib, or .ris file. Each reference is validated against Crossref; references with missing or broken DOIs are matched by title. Requires an internet connection.

.txt: one reference per line. .bib / .ris: entries are split automatically.

      Journal & Venue Formatter

      Choose a target venue to get an interactive checklist of its documented formatting rules, a visual preview of a manuscript excerpt styled to match, a downloadable .docx template, and a quick compliance check on pasted text. Rules are transcribed from each venue's official guidelines — the source and last-checked date are shown below the selector, and details a venue does not publish are marked not specified rather than guessed. Everything runs offline once the page has loaded, except the one-time fetch of the docx.js library for template download.

      Downloadable template

      Generates a .docx with the venue's margins, font, spacing, title page, abstract block, and heading styles pre-configured, plus placeholders and inline notes. Uses docx.js from a pinned CDN (needs an internet connection the first time).

      Quick compliance check

      Paste an abstract or manuscript excerpt. Checks the abstract word limit, keyword count, anonymization red flags (author-identifying material), and heading-pattern hints for the selected venue.

        ScholarFix runs entirely in your browser. Metadata lookups use the free Crossref and OpenAlex APIs; citation formatting uses citation.js and official CSL styles. Always double-check results against the publisher record before submission.
